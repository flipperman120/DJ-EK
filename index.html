The History of DJing

Coining the Term

1935

American radio commentator Walter Winchell officially coins the term "disc jockey" to describe the work of radio announcer Martin Block.

The Twin Turntable

1947

The transition toward continuous play begins to emerge as early setups experiment with dual-turntable units to eliminate silence between records.

The Sound Journey

1960s – 1970s

Club pioneers like Francis Grasso develop early beatmatching, while Jamaican sound systems establish "toasting" and track manipulation techniques.

The Hip-Hop Breakbeat

1973

DJ Kool Herc introduces "The Merry-Go-Round" technique in the Bronx, isolating and looping drum breaks to launch early hip-hop culture.

The Digital Revolution

2010

The era of vinyl and CDs shifts rapidly toward laptops, software sync features, and portable MIDI controllers, changing how music is mixed live globally.

The Next Chapter

2026

Carrying on the core traditions of great track selection, perfect pacing, and crowd-hype performance into modern spaces.
