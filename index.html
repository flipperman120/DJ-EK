Evolution of the Beat

Coining the Term

1935

Commentator Walter Winchell originates the phrase "disc jockey," forever capturing the skill of blending radio broadcast tracks live.

The Dual Deck

1947

Early innovators connect double-turntable setups, pioneering continuous play layouts engineered to kill dead air permanently.

The Club Pioneers

1960s – 1970s

Underground heavyweights engineer early beatmatching while global sound systems perfect track manipulation styles.

The Breakbeat

1973

DJ Kool Herc introduces the legendary "Merry-Go-Round" loop sequence in the Bronx, laying down the structural foundation for hip-hop.

Digital Dominance

2010

The vinyl ecosystem scales into MIDI controllers and software sync nodes, reconstructing live audio mixing for mobile setups.

The Modern Frontier

2026

We take traditional selector roots and cross-fuse them with pristine digital systems to deliver premium crowd control.
